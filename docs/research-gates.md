# Problem Solving & Quant Research Gates

## 1. Epistemic Claim Taxonomy

Every technical or research claim in `Alpha_mining` MUST be classified honestly:

| Claim Kind | Meaning | Allowed Evidence Status Ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| `fact` | Directly observed in code, logs, dataset, or verified tool output | Up to `independently_replicated` |
| `assumption` | Provisional premise declared explicitly before solving | `proposed` / `exploratory` |
| `hypothesis` | Falsifiable statement about mechanism or edge | `proposed` / `exploratory` |
| `inference` | Reasoned deduction from named facts and assumptions | `exploratory` / `reproducible` |
| `computed_result` | Output generated by Solver script before independent countercheck | Up to `reproducible` |
| `independently_verified_result` | Output reproduced and challenged by separate Verifier/Reviewer/Auditor | Up to `independently_replicated` |

## 2. Evidence Status Ladder

Report status **per specific claim or experiment**, never as a blanket endorsement of the whole repository:

1. **`proposed`** — Framing & falsifiable hypothesis written; no implementation or test run yet.
2. **`exploratory`** — Initial prototype or single-pass in-sample experiment; high overfitting risk; never deployable.
3. **`reproducible`** — Deterministic code, pinned inputs/versions, explicit seed/environment, and documented reproduction steps pass independent re-execution.
4. **`robustness_validated`** — Survives Out-of-Sample testing, cost/slippage stress, parameter sensitivity, and leakage/multiple-testing checks.
5. **`independently_replicated`** — Verified end-to-end by an independent implementation/verifier and audited against adversarial counterexamples.

## 3. Quant Research Pre-Intake Checklist

Before `ConditioningBrief` (`domain: quant_research`) can initiate an OVCA workflow:
1. **Economic Rationale**: Why should this inefficiency or risk premium exist structurally (microstructure, inventory risk, behavioral flow, liquidity provision), and who is on the other side?
2. **Falsifiable Hypothesis & Alternatives**: What specific observation rejects the hypothesis, and what alternative explanations (regime noise, volatility clustering, survivorship/lookahead bias) could explain the same signal?
3. **Cost & Microstructure Model**: Commissions, bid-ask spread, market impact, borrow/funding, latency assumptions.
4. **Leakage & Multiple Testing Controls**: Strict temporal split (`embargo`/`purged` splits where applicable), zero future-bar leakage, tracking total trials (`Deflated Sharpe` / Bonferroni / FDR awareness).
5. **Solver ≠ Verifier Separation**: Distinct `solver_actor`, `verifier_actor`/`reviewer_actor`, and `auditor_actor`.

## 4. Proof Levels (Do Not Conflate)

- **Valid Candidate**: Satisfies format/constraints without runtime errors.
- **Correctness**: Matches mathematical/logic specification on verified test cases and invariants.
- **Completeness**: Covers all edge cases, regimes, or constraint space specified.
- **Optimality**: Proven or empirically established best within a stated objective & constraint class.
- **Uniqueness**: Proven no distinct alternative yields the same optimal solution.
